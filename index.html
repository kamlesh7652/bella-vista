About Bella Vista

Our Story

Set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Shivalik hills, our lush garden area offers a truly enchanting venue for life’s most memorable occasions. Whether you’re planning a dream wedding, a private celebration, or a corporate gathering under the stars, the space is designed to inspire and impress. Surrounded by nature’s beauty and supported by Bellavista’s signature personalized service, your event becomes more than just a function — it becomes a timeless experience. From floral arrangements to customized setups, we ensure every detail reflects your vision.

Among our most popular attractions is the Sunset Cinema — an open-air theater experience under the stars. Loved by guests and locals alike, it features two movie screenings every weekend, offering the perfect way to end your day with laughter, nostalgia, or adventure.
